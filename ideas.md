## Option A — "Dark Atelier" (Chosen)

**Design Movement:** Dark Luxury Artisan — inspired by high-end jewelry boutiques and art galleries

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep obsidian backgrounds with gold foil accents — the art glows against darkness
2. Asymmetric editorial layouts with generous whitespace
3. Tactile, handcrafted feel through organic shapes and flowing curves
4. Every section feels like opening a luxury gift box

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep charcoal/obsidian `#0d0d0d` / `#111111`
- Primary Gold: Rich warm gold `#C9A84C` / `#D4AF37`
- Pearl White: Soft ivory `#F5F0E8`
- Accent: Iridescent shimmer hints (CSS gradients)
- Text: Warm cream `#EDE8DC`

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with parallax resin art imagery
- Staggered product grid (masonry-style, not uniform)
- Diagonal section dividers (clip-path) for visual flow
- Sticky navigation with glass morphism effect

**Signature Elements:**
1. Gold shimmer dividers between sections (animated gradient lines)
2. Product cards with hover "resin pour" reveal effect
3. Floating gold particle animations in hero

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals are slow and luxurious (400ms ease)
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins for product reveals
- Gold shimmer on CTA buttons

**Animation:**
- Hero: Slow Ken Burns zoom on background image
- Products: Staggered entrance on scroll (framer-motion)
- Nav: Frosted glass blur on scroll
- Buttons: Gold shimmer sweep on hover

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Cormorant Garamond` — elegant, editorial serif for headings
- Body: `Jost` — clean geometric sans for readability
- Accent: `Great Vibes` — flowing script for taglines/quotes
